    How I made it

    I wrote this as a continuing series so each bedtime adventure could build on familiar places and characters. I used ElevenLabs Creative Flows to narrate the stories in parts, then joined those parts into a single track for each chapter.

    The episode list comes from a catalog. Future stories can be added by publishing an MP3 and appending its details to the catalog.
